# Third-Party Licenses

No third-party art packs are currently vendored in this vault.

`tools/pack_cc0_themes.py` only swaps theme atlases when verified CC0/MIT source images
exist under `vendor/cc0_topdown/`. Candidate upstreams (to be added when reachable and
license-verified): Kenney CC0 Topdown Shooter / Industrial / Sci-Fi (`https://kenney.nl/assets`,
CC0 1.0), 0x72 CC0 tilesets (`https://0x72.itch.io`, CC0 1.0).

Until a source is verified and recorded here, all `themes/<theme_id>/` atlases remain
**ORIGINAL_PROCEDURAL** (see `PROVENANCE.md`). No unclear-license art is redistributed.

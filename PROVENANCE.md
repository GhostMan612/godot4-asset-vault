# Asset Provenance

All theme atlases under `themes/<theme_id>/` are **ORIGINAL_PROCEDURAL**, generated by
`tools/build_theme_packs.py` with deterministic Pillow drawing (no third-party content).

Other packs in this vault originate from CC0 / MIT sources recorded in `manifest.json`
and their per-pack manifests. No proprietary textures are redistributed here.
